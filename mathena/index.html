MathSpeak Help

How to say an equation so it converts cleanly, and how to move around it once it's on screen.

How it works

1 · ListenThe browser's speech recognition turns your voice into words.
2 · TranslateClaude converts the words to LaTeX. A built-in rule parser covers common phrases when Claude isn't available.
3 · RenderMathJax draws the LaTeX. The page also parses it into a tree of layers and rebuilds the clean spoken text from that tree.
4 · NavigateEach layer is tagged in all three views, so selecting one lights up the same layer everywhere.

Scope cues

Say these to mark where a group ends
  • “the quantity … close quantity”Parentheses around a group
  • “square root of … end root”Everything under the root
  • “… all over …”Puts everything said so far on top of a fraction
  • “to the power of … end exponent”A longer exponent
  • “integral from a to b of … d x”Definite integral with limits
  • “sum from i equals 1 to n of …”Summation with limits

Voice commands

The mic turns on when the page opens and waits for a command. Say each command on its own, with a short pause before it
  • “Start new”Dictate a new equation (also “Start listening”)
  • “Start edit”Read back the selected part of the current equation and say a change to it
  • “Go to export”, “Help”, “Go to workspace”Switch pages; works from any page
  • “Pause”Stop adding words; the mic keeps listening for commands
  • “Done”Convert what you said (or apply the change you said)
  • “Clear”Empty the box you are dictating into
  • “Select that”Read back the selected layer and edit only that part
  • “Out a layer”, “In a layer”, “Previous”, “Next”Move around the equation
  • “Delete”Remove the selected layer
  • “Say a change”, then “Refine with AI”Describe an edit to the selected layer and let Claude apply it
  • “Refine manually”Open the LaTeX to edit it by hand
  • “Save”Add the current equation to the Export list
  • “Stop listening”Stop dictating; the mic stays on for commands
  • “Turn off the mic”Turn the mic off completely

Moving through layers

  • ↑ Out a layerSelect the group that contains this part
  • ↓ In a layerSelect the first part inside this one
  • ← → Previous, nextMove to a neighbor in the same layer
  • EscBack to the whole equation
Dictation uses your browser's speech recognition (Chrome or Edge work best). If the microphone is blocked here, open the page as a standalone file, or type instead.
