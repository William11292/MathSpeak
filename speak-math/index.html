    How it works

    1 · ListenThe browser's speech recognition turns your voice into words.
    2 · TranslateClaude converts the words to LaTeX. A built-in rule parser covers common phrases when Claude isn't available.
    3 · RenderMathJax draws the LaTeX. The page also parses it into a tree of layers and rebuilds the clean spoken text from that tree.
    4 · Explore and logEach layer is tagged in all three results, so hovering one lights up the same layer everywhere. Every result is logged.
    Dictation uses your browser's speech recognition (Chrome or Edge work best). If the microphone is blocked here, open the page as a standalone file, or type instead.
